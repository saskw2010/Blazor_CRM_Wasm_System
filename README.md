> A modern Single Page Application (SPA) Customer Relationship Management (CRM) & Sales pipeline tracker built on **Blazor WebAssembly (WASM)** and **Radzen Blazor UI**.

---

## 🌟 Capabilities & Features

- 💼 **Customer & Lead Tracking**: Manage customer accounts, contact pipelines, and deal stages.
- 📊 **Sales Pipeline Analytics**: Interactive charts and Radzen DataGrid summaries of sales conversion rates.
- ⚡ **Client-Side WASM Execution**: High-speed offline-capable WebAssembly client rendering with minimal server load.
